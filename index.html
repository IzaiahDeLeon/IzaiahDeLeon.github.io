Izaiah DeLeon

Water Safety Instructor📚

My name is Izaiah DeLeon, and I am a member of the Fontana Park Aquatics Department in Fontana, California. I have spent the past three years working in aquatics, developing my skills, gaining valuable experience, and building a passion for water safety and education. Throughout my time with the department, I have had the opportunity to take on different responsibilities, grow professionally, and make a positive impact on the community. I began my career as a lifeguard, a position I held for two years. During that time, I developed a strong understanding of water safety, emergency preparedness, facility operations, and the importance of maintaining a safe and welcoming environment for the public. Working as a lifeguard taught me the value of responsibility, attentiveness, teamwork, and clear communication. It also helped me understand how important aquatics professionals are in creating an environment where individuals and families can enjoy the water safely.

After two years as a lifeguard, I transitioned into the role of Water Safety Instructor, where I have spent the past year teaching children of different ages, abilities, and experience levels how to swim. I have had the opportunity to work with students at various stages of their swimming development, from beginners learning the fundamentals of water safety and basic swimming skills to more experienced swimmers building their confidence and improving their techniques. This experience has been especially rewarding because I get to witness students overcome challenges, develop new abilities, and become more comfortable in the water. As an instructor, I believe patience, encouragement, and adaptability are essential to creating a positive learning experience. Every student learns differently, and I strive to recognize each child's individual needs while providing instruction that is both effective and enjoyable. Beyond teaching swimming techniques, I believe one of the most important parts of my job is helping children develop confidence, independence, and a lasting respect for water safety.

I take pride in being part of a department that serves the Fontana community and provides opportunities for people to learn valuable lifelong skills. My experience as both a lifeguard and Water Safety Instructor has helped me develop professionally while strengthening my communication, leadership, problem-solving, and interpersonal skills. I look forward to continuing to grow in the aquatics field, expanding my knowledge, and making a meaningful difference in the lives of the individuals and families I serve.
